**Learning** is the current workspace's study area. Open it from the sidebar to create an activity, search existing records, or continue where you stopped. In the Classic layout this area is called **Personalized Learning**.

![Learning activities in one workspace](/screenshots/dev/learning.png)

## Choose a way to learn

| Activity | Start with | What you keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Books](/explore/book/) | A subject and optional source material | An editable chapter plan, generated pages, reading position and page conversations |
| [Mastery Path](/explore/mastery-path/) | A goal describing what you want to be able to do | A knowledge-point map, topic conversations and mastery evidence |
| [Practice](/explore/practice/) | Collected or imported questions | Answers, mistakes, recall ratings and next review dates |
| [Immersive Reading](/explore/reading/) | A document, web page, video or audio source | A material collection, source-linked conversations, highlights and notes |

Books build a resource to read; Mastery Path tracks abilities; Practice exercises recall; Immersive Reading stays close to an existing source. Pick the one that fits the task, then move between them as needed.

## Start and resume

1. Check the selected [workspace](/explore/workspaces/).
2. Use the create control in Learning or the sidebar's new-learning entry. Choose an activity and provide its topic, goal or materials.
3. Reopen the resulting record from **All learning** or the feature's own library.
4. Filter by activity type or search by title when the list grows. **Show more** reveals older records.

The overview orders activity by recent updates. Books show reading progress; paths show mastered knowledge points. **What needs your attention** highlights due path reviews and books still compiling or paused. A paused book needs inspection before you treat it as complete. If one source fails to load, retry that source; an incomplete overview does not mean your records were deleted.

## Turn a chapter into lasting knowledge

For a probability exam, import your lecture PDF into Reading and ask about a difficult proof. Use the clarified objective to create a Mastery Path. Save a concise explanation to Favorites and insert it into your Learning notebook beside your own worked example. Review collected questions in Practice the next day. You can also generate a Book when you need a coherent account of the whole subject.

[Resources](/explore/space/) holds the reusable inputs and saved results. **Learning journal** records the current mission, session handoff and confirmed learning records; it complements individual activity progress.

## Access and scope

Activities depend on the modules, models and account permissions available in your deployment. A locked or missing feature may require administrator access or module setup. Learning records belong to the active workspace; switching workspace changes the library. Partners and Personalization remain available across workspaces. In shared deployments, administrators manage the model catalog and user grants govern model access.
