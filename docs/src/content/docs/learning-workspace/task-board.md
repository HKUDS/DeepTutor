Open **Home → Task Board** (`/kanban`) to organize the active workspace's tasks. Use it for concrete actions such as “Attempt five integration problems” or “Summarize chapter 4,” while Learning keeps the books, paths and reading activities those actions refer to.

![Task Board](/screenshots/dev/task-board.png)

## Plan and update work

1. Enter a task title and add it to the board.
2. Open the card to edit its title, note and optional due date.
3. Move it through the board's pending, in-progress and completed columns. You can drag cards or use each card's movement controls.
4. Filter to **Today** or **This week** to narrow the board by date.
5. Archive completed tasks when you want to clear the active view. Use the archived view to restore them.

A card's due date appears on the card; overdue unfinished work is highlighted. Cards can also show how many calendar time blocks are linked to them. Open [Calendar](/learning-workspace/calendar/) to plan time around the work. A deadline and a reserved study period are distinct: giving a task a due date does not by itself mean you reserved time to do it.

## A practical revision board

Create three tasks: “Read the conditional-probability proof,” “Write my own example,” and “Complete today's review.” Keep the reading collection, notebook page and Practice session in their own learning surfaces; put their next actions on the board. Move each card as you finish it, and archive the set after the exam.

The board follows the selected [workspace](/learning-workspace/workspaces/). If you switch from `Probability` to `Research project`, you see that project's tasks. Calendar events remain account level, with workspace task and study layers shown alongside them.
