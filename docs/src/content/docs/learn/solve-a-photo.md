Use this lesson when the problem is on a page, a worksheet, or a photo, and you want to follow the work line by line. You attach one cropped picture. The reply puts numbered steps and a final answer in the thread, next to that picture.

<aside class="dt-prereq">
<p class="dt-prereq-title">Before you begin</p>
<p>Open the app and connect one language model. If this computer does not have that yet, start from the <a href="/get-started/desktop/">desktop quickstart</a>.</p>
</aside>

![Choose Solve, open attach, and type the request](/gifs/learn-solve-a-photo.gif)

The clip opens **Mode**, chooses **Solve**, opens **Resources**, and types the request. You still attach the cropped photo, then send.

## 1. Open chat in the subject workspace

Open [Chat](/learning-workspace/chat/) in the workspace for this subject.

You are in that workspace's chat.

## 2. Choose Solve

Open **Mode** and choose **Solve**. Solve writes the problem out as steps you can check.

The message shows **Solve**.

## 3. Attach one cropped photo

Open **Resources** (`+`) and attach one photo of the problem. Crop it to the question you want solved.

The photo is on the message.

## 4. Ask for steps and a final answer

Send: “Solve this. Show each step, then the final answer. Stop and ask if the figure is ambiguous.”

Wait until the reply finishes. The thread shows numbered steps and a final answer, with the photo still on your message.

## 5. Check the steps against the photo

Read each step next to the photo.

You can see which step matches the picture and which step does not. This lesson ends there.
