You turn a problem on paper into steps you can check against the picture. You attach one cropped photo of that problem. The reply puts the steps and a final answer in the thread. Choose this when the problem is on a page, a worksheet, or a photo, and you want to follow each line of the work. You leave with those steps on screen beside the photo. You can see which step matches the picture and which step does not.

<aside class="dt-prereq">
<p class="dt-prereq-title">Before you begin</p>
<p>Open the app and connect one language model. If this computer does not have that yet, start from the <a href="/get-started/desktop/">desktop quickstart</a>.</p>
</aside>

## 1. Open chat in the subject workspace

Open [Chat](/learning-workspace/chat/) in the workspace for this subject.

The composer is ready in that workspace.

## 2. Choose Solve

In the capability menu, open **More Capabilities** and choose **Solve**. A capability is the workflow for this turn.

**Solve** is selected on the message you are about to send.

## 3. Attach one cropped photo

Use **+** and attach one photo of the problem. Crop it to the question you want solved.

The photo is attached to the message.

## 4. Ask for steps and a final answer

Send: “Solve this. Show each step, then the final answer. Stop and ask if the figure is ambiguous.”

Wait until the reply finishes. The thread shows numbered steps and a final answer.

## 5. Check the steps against the photo

Read each step next to the photo.

The steps and the photo are both on screen.

You are done when the thread has steps you can check against the photo.
