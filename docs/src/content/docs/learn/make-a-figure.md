A relationship that is hard to follow in a sentence becomes one figure in the thread. You describe that relationship and ask Visualize to draw it. Visualize is the workflow that turns one description into a figure you can read in the chat. This lesson uses Visualize only. Choose this when you want the shape, the axes, or the comparison in front of you while you keep reading the thread. You leave with that one figure in the thread.

<aside class="dt-prereq">
<p class="dt-prereq-title">Before you begin</p>
<p>Open the app and connect one language model. If this computer does not have that yet, start from the <a href="/get-started/desktop/">desktop quickstart</a>.</p>
</aside>

## 1. Open chat

Open [Chat](/learning-workspace/chat/).

The composer is ready.

## 2. Choose Visualize

Choose **Visualize** in the capability menu. A capability is the workflow for this turn. Visualize turns one description into a figure in the thread.

**Visualize** is selected for this message.

## 3. Ask for one figure

Send: “Draw a sine wave I can read. Label the axes.”

Your request is in the thread.

## 4. Read the figure

Wait until the figure renders in the thread. It may be SVG, Mermaid, Chart.js, or HTML.

You are done when that figure appears in the thread.
